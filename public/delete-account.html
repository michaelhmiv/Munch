Account control

Delete your Munch account

Account deletion is permanent and removes your personal Munch account data from the primary application database, subject to the limited retention described in the Privacy Policy.

Delete from Munch

  1. Sign in to Munch using the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and the account/data controls.
  3. Choose Delete account, enter the required confirmation phrase, and confirm the permanent deletion.

The same deletion controls are available from the installed Munch mobile app after sign-in.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes the active Munch account, personal nutrition records, saved foods, drafts, preferences, and active connections from the primary application database. Shared household attribution and limited security, backup, billing, fraud, accounting, legal, or dispute records may be retained only as described in the Munch Privacy Policy.

Deleting Munch does not delete conversations held by ChatGPT/OpenAI. An active subscription should also be canceled with the billing provider if you do not want future renewal charges.

Need help?

If you cannot access the account, contact support@munch.business from the account email address. Do not send nutrition records or passwords by email.
