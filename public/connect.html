Claude — exact steps

  1. Open Claude and go to Customize → Connectors (or Settings → Connectors).
  2. Choose +, then Add custom connector.
  3. Enter Name: Munch and Server URL: https://munch.business/mcp, then choose Continue. Leave Advanced OAuth fields empty — Munch registers automatically.
  4. Review the detected sign-in settings, then choose Add.
  5. Choose Connect — enter your email on the Munch page, open the link sent to you, and approve.
  6. Return to Claude, turn Munch on in your chat, and ask: “Log my lunch.”

ChatGPT — exact steps

  1. On ChatGPT on the web, open Settings → Apps & Connectors.
  2. Under Advanced settings, turn on Developer mode. (Your AI cannot flip this switch for you.)
  3. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create.
  4. Enter Name: Munch, Server URL: https://munch.business/mcp, and Authentication: OAuth. Confirm and choose Create.
  5. In the Munch page that opens, enter your email, open the link sent to you, and approve.
  6. Return to ChatGPT, enable Munch from the + menu, and start logging.

Muse — exact steps

  1. In your terminal, add Munch as a remote MCP server:
    claude mcp add --transport http Munch https://munch.business/mcp
  2. Start Muse, run /mcp, then choose Munch and Authenticate.
  3. In the Munch page that opens, enter your email, open the sign-in link, and approve.
  4. Return to Muse: /mcp shows Munch connected with all 95 tools available.

Any MCP app — exact steps

  1. Open your app's settings and look for Custom connector, MCP server, or Remote server.
  2. Add a server with Name: Munch and Server URL: https://munch.business/mcp.
  3. Choose OAuth for authentication and start the connection.
  4. In the Munch page that opens, enter your email, open the link sent to you, and approve.
  5. Return to your app and confirm Munch shows as connected.

Troubleshooting & permissions

What am I granting?

The approval page lists the exact permissions requested. Approving lets that app call Munch tools for your account only — Munch never receives unrelated conversations.

The email link did not arrive

Check spam and filtered folders, verify the address, then request a fresh link. Links are single-use and expire; a confirmation tap on the Munch page stops email scanners from consuming your link first.

“This request expired”

Authorization requests are short-lived. Return to your AI app and choose Connect again to start a fresh request rather than reusing an old browser tab.

How to revoke access

Open Munch Settings → Connections and revoke the client. Access stops immediately without deleting your account or nutrition records.
