Privacy by product design

No advertising analytics. No separate AI model.

ChatGPT interprets your conversation. Munch authenticates the request, retrieves food data, validates the record, and stores only what is needed to provide the service.

Operational boundaries

No Google Analytics or advertising trackers. Nutrition records are isolated by account and are not exposed through normal billing or support screens. You can export or delete your data from Munch.

Munch began as a fork of Nutrition MCP by Alexander Kutishevsky. The original project is MIT-licensed. Munch is an independent product and is not presented as an endorsement by the original developer.

Questions

What people need to know.

Does Munch run its own AI model?

No. ChatGPT handles language and image understanding. Munch provides the authenticated tools, food-source lookups, deterministic validation, and nutrition storage.

Can Munch see my conversations?

Munch only receives the tool arguments ChatGPT sends when it calls Munch. It does not receive unrelated conversation content. Tool requests can include nutrition details needed to perform the requested action.

What happens when a food match is uncertain?

Munch can create a draft, ask follow-up questions, show assumptions, and require confirmation before the meal becomes part of your history.

Can I move or delete my data?

Yes. Munch supports export and permanent account deletion. Active MCP connections can also be revoked from the account portal.

Is Munch medical advice?

No. Munch is a consumer wellness and tracking product. Nutrition estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate and should not replace a clinician or registered dietitian.
