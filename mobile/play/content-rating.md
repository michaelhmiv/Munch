# Google Play content rating working answers

Use this as a preparation checklist only; answer the current IARC/Play Console questionnaire exactly as worded at submission time.

Expected release characteristics:

- No violence or graphic injury content.
- No sexual content or nudity.
- No profanity as an app feature.
- No gambling or simulated gambling.
- No controlled-substance sales or promotion.
- No alcohol/tobacco sales. Munch can optionally record alcohol nutrition consumed by the user; it does not sell or encourage alcohol use.
- No user-to-user messaging, public social feed, or public user-generated-content browsing.
- Household collaboration is private to invited members and concerns recipes/plans/groceries/Pantry records.
- AI-generated Pantry meal suggestions are private to the requesting account/workspace and include an in-app report control.
- No advertising in the certified Android release.
- No location sharing.
- No web browser/general open-web browsing feature.

If the questionnaire asks whether users can exchange content, distinguish invited private household records from a public UGC/social platform and answer the exact question rather than relying on this summary.
