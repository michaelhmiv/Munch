## Full description

Munch brings meal tracking, nutrition history, recipes, meal planning, grocery lists and Pantry inventory into one connected workspace.

LOG FOOD WITHOUT STARTING OVER
• Search generic and branded foods.
• Scan supported food barcodes.
• Log meals and reusable foods.
• Track calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and additional nutrients.
• Record water and body weight.
• Review nutrition history and trends.

PLAN WHAT TO EAT
• Save and organize recipes.
• Import supported recipe URLs.
• Schedule meals on your plan.
• Build grocery lists from recipes and planning.
• Keep personal and shared household planning separate where appropriate.

KNOW WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
Premium Pantry can keep track of food in your pantry, refrigerator and freezer. Add items manually, scan a food barcode, or choose a Pantry or receipt photo for review before anything changes your inventory.

When enabled, Munch can generate grounded meal ideas from the Pantry and saved recipes you choose to use. AI-assisted suggestions are estimates and can be reported from inside the app.

ONE MUNCH ACCOUNT
The Android app uses the same Munch account and records as munch.business and supported Munch connections. Existing website subscribers keep their eligible Munch Premium access after signing in. Android users can also subscribe to personal Munch Premium through Google Play where available.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
• No advertising SDKs in the certified Android release.
• Raw Pantry and receipt photos are processed for the requested scan and are not stored as account media in the Munch database.
• Export and permanent account-deletion controls are available from Munch settings.
• Munch does not sell nutrition data for advertising.

Munch is a consumer wellness and nutrition-tracking product. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Nutrition values and AI-assisted estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
